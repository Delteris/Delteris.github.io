Zero-Friction Work Capture

Log work in 10 seconds or less — welding, fit-up, cutting or any custom type. Offline-first, so the floor keeps recording when the connection drops.

All the details
  • 10-Second Navigation: A streamlined mobile-first interface gets operators to the right project step and inputting data in 10 seconds or less.
  • Universal Work Templates: Track more than welding. Define custom work types — fit-up, cutting, assembly — save them as organization-wide templates, and capture any floor activity without slowing the team down.
  • Pragmatic Parameter Tracking: Logs custom parameters and dates instantly, without bottlenecking the floor with digital signatures for routine entries.
  • Offline-First Work Capture (Standard): Capture is built to run disconnected — welders record work with no connection at all, not just when it happens to drop. Included on Standard; the offline document viewer stays on every plan.

One Project, Two Live Views

Edit projects as a Tree or a Flowchart. Paste a BOM from Tekla or most CAD packages and the structure is built in seconds.

04

Qualifications & Procedures

Automatic 6-month continuity tracking, an end-to-end WPS/WPQR workflow, live WPQR capture on the floor, and heat propagation down the project tree.

All the details
  • Qualification Engine: Deep tracking for welder and non-welder qualifications. Automatically logs 6-month continuity from actual floor entries and warns before expirations.
  • End-to-End WPS/WPQR Workflow: Define a preliminary WPS in the app, capture actual WPQR parameters on the shop floor, import real values back, adjust ranges, and export a finalized WPS — with official files uploaded or linked at any stage.
  • Automatic WPS Import: A parser reads your existing WPS documents (Excel or exported PDF — not scans) and extracts every field automatically — no re-typing. The same engine powers Elenchon Atlas, our standalone cloud WPS library.
  • Live WPQR Capture: Capture Welding Procedure Qualification Record parameters on the floor and see them reflected in the management app instantly.
  • Fast Heat Propagation: Heat numbers are captured, linked to their parent MTRs, and automatically propagated down the project tree — full traceability without database bloat.
  • Versioned as Captured: Every document, parameter, and consumable is versioned and verified the moment it is captured. Corrections are versioned too and stay visible to managers — a clean history without an administrative bottleneck.

Questions & Answers

Welding Management Software, Answered

What is welding management software?

Welding management software digitizes a fabrication shop's welding records — welder qualifications (WPS, WPQR, WPQ), weld tracking and traceability, procedure control and inspection history — so every joint is traceable and audit-ready. Elenchon does this entirely on-premise, keeping the full weld record on hardware you own.

See full weld traceability →

Is Elenchon cloud-based or on-premise?

Elenchon is a 100% on-premise appliance installed in your facility — the sovereign alternative to cloud welding software (see the honest comparison). Your manufacturing data never touches a shared server, there are no forced updates, and you don't depend on a vendor's cloud to reach your own records.

How data sovereignty works →

Is Elenchon just weld traceability, or does it run qualification too?
Both, in one system. Alongside traceability, Elenchon runs the full WPS/WPQR/WPQ workflow — live WPQR capture on the floor, automatic six-month continuity and expiry alerts — so a requalification is never missed and no welder works outside an approved range. See the qualification workflow →

How does it help with ISO 3834 and ASME IX compliance?

Every qualification, variable and process is verified and attributed the moment it is captured, so audit evidence for codes such as ISO 3834 and ASME IX is instant and undisputed.

See how audit evidence works →

Can we access an on-premise system remotely?
Yes, through the optional Remote Access Module. Authorised staff and auditors connect from anywhere — mobile data included — over Phrakton’s mutually authenticated, post-quantum-safe tunnels, while the appliance opens no inbound ports and stays invisible to the public internet.
