Control That Doesn't Turn Into Clutter

Welding QMS software runs the quality-management side of a fabrication shop — who is authorised to do what, which documents are produced, and how records are controlled and versioned. A welding quality-management system is only as good as the people who can actually use it. Elenchon's management app gives the office top-down control over roles, permissions, documents and alerts — and keeps every screen clean by hiding whatever a given user has no business seeing. Access is managed once, globally, instead of project by project, so control scales as the operation grows rather than turning into administrative sprawl. It is the sovereign, on-premise alternative to cloud welding software.

Access Control

Global Custom Roles

Define who can do what, once, for the whole organization.

Every shop divides responsibility differently. Elenchon lets you model your own structure with fully custom roles, managed centrally.

  • Fully custom roles: Create the exact roles your operation uses and assign permissions to match, instead of bending to fixed tiers.
  • Top-down, not per-project: Manage access globally and eliminate project-by-project permission headaches.
  • Anti-bloat interface: The management app automatically hides any feature or data a user lacks permission to see, so every screen stays clean and free of clutter.
Document Control

One-Click Document Generation

Turn captured data into your own paperwork, instantly.

Auditors and customers want documents in your format, not a generic export. Elenchon pulls live data straight into the templates you already use.

  • Your templates, filled automatically: Pull captured node data straight into your company's paperwork templates for immediate printing.
  • Reusable everywhere: Use a template across one project or the entire organization — build it once, apply it anywhere.
  • Version-pinned records: Every document, parameter and consumable is versioned the moment it is captured and locked against direct editing — strict compliance with no bottleneck.
Right People, Right Alerts

Role-Filtered Notifications

Keep leaders informed without spamming every phone on the floor.

An alert everyone gets is an alert no one reads. Elenchon routes warnings to exactly the people who need them.

  • Filtered by role: An in-app dashboard filters critical warnings — qualification expirations, maintenance — strictly by role.
  • Signal, not noise: Leaders stay informed of what matters to them, while the floor isn't buried in irrelevant pings.
  • Live project editing, protected: Managers modify in-progress projects while the system automatically locks any node that already contains captured work.
Audit Readiness

A Quality System Auditors Can Follow

Roles, documents and records all resolve into version-pinned ledgers, verified and attributed the moment they are captured. That gives you a defensible quality-management trail for codes such as ISO 3834, EN 1090 and ASME IX — who was authorised, what document was produced, and when — instant and undisputed when the auditors arrive.

On-Premise, Not Cloud

Your Quality Records Never Leave the Shop

Most QMS and document-control tools are cloud tools: your roles, templates and records live on someone else's server, behind someone else's outages. Elenchon is the opposite — a containerized appliance installed in your facility, fully under your jurisdiction.

  • Absolute data ownership: Quality records stay on your physical hardware. Cancel anytime and keep permanent view-only access for audits.
  • No forced updates, no cloud dependency: A vendor cloud outage can never lock you out of your own quality system — it lives on your appliance, not someone else's server.
  • Secure remote access, optional: Reach the app from home or the field through Phrakton, a zero-trust, post-quantum-safe overlay — while the appliance opens no inbound ports to the public internet.
