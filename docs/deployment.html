How It Works

From First Call to Live Floor

A structured, low-friction path — your production never stops while we work.

1

Scoping Session

A focused on-site visit — typically about half a day — to map your project structures, compliance requirements and floor workflows, and confirm Elenchon fits. A separate trip on Standard; Lite goes straight to installation, which is why it costs less.

2

Your Templates, Made Elastic

We turn the paperwork you already use into elastic templates, so the app arrives producing your forms, in your format. Kept confidential and used only for you.

3

On-Site Installation

Our team delivers and configures the hardware-secured server appliance directly at your facility. A formal agreement guarantees absolute legal and industrial data confidentiality.

4

Onboarding & Go-Live

We set the app up with you — configuring roles alongside your managers, tailoring it to how your floor works, and training your personnel — so your team is productive from day one. Production continues uninterrupted throughout.

Zero Lock-In Risk

Your data lives on your hardware from day one. Cancel any time and keep view-only access to every historical record for audits, plus the database and backups to use as you wish. No hostage data, ever.

Full Attention, Not Volume

Our own engineers run every deployment on-site, start to finish — we're based in Portugal and travel to install. To hold that standard we cap deployments per quarter. Join the launch list early to reserve the quarter that suits you.
