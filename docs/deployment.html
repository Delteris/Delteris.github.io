How It Works

From First Call to Live Floor

A structured, low-friction path — your production never stops while we work.

1

Scoping Session

A focused on-site visit from our team to map your project structures, compliance requirements, and floor workflows — and confirm Elenchon fits your operation. It takes as long as the work needs, typically about half a day. Standard includes this as a separate trip; Lite folds straight to installation, which is why it costs less.

2

Your Templates, Made Elastic

While we scope your setup, we adapt the paperwork you already use into elastic templates and build them into your deployment — so the app arrives already producing your forms, in your format. Fully classified and used only for you.

3

On-Site Installation

Our team delivers and configures the hardware-secured server appliance directly at your facility. A formal agreement guarantees absolute legal and industrial data confidentiality.

4

Onboarding & Go-Live

We configure roles with your managers and train floor personnel, so your team is productive from day one — production continues uninterrupted throughout.

Zero Lock-In Risk

Your data lives on your hardware from day one. Cancel at any time and retain view-only access to all historical records for audits on the app — no hostage data, ever. You also retain access to the database and backups to use them at your own will.

Full Attention, Not Volume

Every deployment is run on-site by our own engineers, start to finish — we're based in Portugal and travel to install. We cap the number per quarter to hold that standard, so the limit reflects capacity, not demand. Contact us early to reserve a slot for the quarter that suits you.
