Equipment Register

Welding Equipment Management

One register for every machine on the floor — welding gear and everything around it.

Power sources, positioners, ovens, cranes, jigs and hand tools all carry records that have to be found on demand. Elenchon holds them in a single, structured register instead of a drawer of binders.

  • Universal machine tracking: Register any asset — not just welding gear — each with its own documents, schedules and status.

Calibration & Certification

Machine Calibration & Certification Tracking

Know that every machine is calibrated, certified and in date — before it goes on a job, not after.

Codes expect the equipment used for welding to be calibrated, verified and validated on a schedule. Elenchon tracks that lifecycle automatically, so nothing runs past its due date unnoticed.

  • Per-machine schedules: Each asset has its own calibration and certification cadence, with the supporting documents pinned to the record.
  • Automated renewal alerts: The system warns you before a calibration or certificate lapses, filtered by role so the right people act in time.
  • ISO 17662 aligned: Supports the calibration, verification and validation of equipment used for welding, and the equipment evidence expected under ISO 3834.
  • Version-pinned history: Every calibration record is locked the moment it is captured, giving auditors an undisputed history rather than an editable spreadsheet.
Consumables & Materials

Consumable Inventory & Filler-Metal Traceability

Trace every filler metal and consumable batch from goods-in to the finished weld.

Consumables carry storage rules, reheat requirements and heat numbers that have to be traceable to the weld they ended up in. Elenchon captures that chain at batch level.

  • Batch-level inventory: Granular logging with customizable storage and reheat rules, plus low-stock alerts so a job never stalls on an empty rod oven.
  • Heat-number traceability: Heat numbers are captured, linked to their parent MTRs, and propagated down the project tree — full material traceability without database bloat.
  • Tied to the weld: Consumable and filler-metal records connect to the captured work, so the chain from certificate to joint is unbroken.
  • Audit-ready by default: Because records are version-pinned on capture, material traceability evidence is prepared before an auditor asks for it.
Maintenance Hub

Shop-Floor Maintenance, Managed

Raise, triage and close maintenance without leaving the floor app.

Broken or overdue equipment is a production and compliance risk. Elenchon turns maintenance into a tracked workflow instead of a shouted request across the shop.

  • Four urgency tiers: Workers raise maintenance requests straight from the floor app, prioritised so the critical ones surface first.
  • End-to-end workflow: Maintenance personnel manage the whole request lifecycle from the management app, with a clear record of what was done and when.
  • Role-sensitive notifications: Expirations and maintenance events are filtered strictly by role, keeping leaders informed without spamming every phone.
  • Offline on the floor: Crucial documents are one click to save for instant access anywhere on the floor, even in total Wi-Fi dead zones.
