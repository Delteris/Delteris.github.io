## Where it stands

Most of Elenchon is built: floor capture, the project tree and flowchart, the WPS, WPQR and welder qualification workflow, roles and permissions, and the Phrakton network it runs on. Launch is planned for Q1 2027.

## What we're changing first

Building Atlas, our cloud product, and showing it to shops has taught us things we want in Elenchon before anyone installs it:

- **Start from what the shop already has.** Atlas reads a shop's existing WPS from Excel and exported PDF. Elenchon is getting the same import, so your WPS library comes in from your own files instead of being typed in.
- **Alerts by urgency, per person.** In Atlas, each person chooses which deadline emails they get: quality hears about everything, production only about what's urgent. Elenchon's notifications will work the same way.
- **Less on screen.** In Atlas, each person can hide the features they don't use. A floor platform has more features, so this matters even more in Elenchon.
