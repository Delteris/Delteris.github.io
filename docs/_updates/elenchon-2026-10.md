## Where it stands

Most of Elenchon is built: floor capture, the project tree and flowchart, the WPS, WPQR and welder qualification workflow, roles and permissions, and the Phrakton network it runs on.

For now, we've paused new Elenchon development and put our time into Atlas, our cloud product. Atlas is smaller and ready to use, and it puts us in front of shops sooner.

## Why Atlas first

Every Atlas release teaches us something about how shops actually work, and we want those lessons in Elenchon before anyone installs it. Three are already on the list:

- **Start from what the shop already has.** Atlas reads a shop's existing WPS from Excel and exported PDF instead of asking anyone to retype them. Elenchon should start from your own files the same way.
- **Alerts by urgency, per person.** In Atlas, each person chooses which deadline emails they get: quality hears about everything, production only about what's urgent.
- **Less on screen.** In Atlas, each person can hide the features they don't use. A floor platform has more features, so this matters even more in Elenchon.

## When

Launch is planned for Q1 2027. If that date moves, you'll hear it here first.
