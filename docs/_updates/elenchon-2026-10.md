## Where it stands

Most of Elenchon is built: floor capture, the project tree and flowchart, the WPS, WPQR and welder qualification workflow, roles and permissions, and the Phrakton network it runs on.

Right now, most of our time goes into Atlas, our cloud product: it's ready to use, and it puts us in front of shops sooner. Elenchon keeps moving alongside it, at a steadier pace.

## Why Atlas first

Every Atlas release teaches us something about how shops actually work, and those lessons go straight into Elenchon. Three are already on the list:

- **Start from what the shop already has.** Atlas reads a shop's existing WPS from Excel and exported PDF instead of asking anyone to retype them. Elenchon should start from your own files the same way.
- **Alerts by urgency, per person.** In Atlas, each person chooses which deadline emails they get: quality hears about everything, production only about what's urgent.
- **Less on screen.** In Atlas, each person can hide the features they don't use. A floor platform has more features, so this matters even more in Elenchon.

## When

Launch is still planned for Q1 2027. If that date moves, you'll hear it here first.

## What this list is for

Subscribe and we'll email you at launch, with the first deployment slots. Each appliance is installed on site by us, so slots per quarter are limited. The [platform page](/platform.html) has everything Elenchon does, and [Atlas](/wps-management-software.html) is available today.
