Elenchon Atlas · Roadmap

What’s Next
in Atlas.

What we’re building for Atlas, in the order it ships. Every release is included in your subscription and explained in the updates when it lands.

About Atlas · Updates · All roadmaps

  1. ShippedOctober 2026

    Atlas 1.2: A WPS Reader That Adapts to Your Forms

    Our first major update. Atlas works out each form’s layout from the file itself, and you can now write, approve and print WPSs in Atlas and confirm many welder qualifications in one round.

    Read the 1.2 notes → All updates →

  2. In developmentNext release · date when it’s close

    Atlas 1.3: Can We Weld This Job?

    At its centre is Project check: the joints a job needs, checked against the WPQRs and welder qualifications you already keep in Atlas. Included for every Atlas customer at no extra cost.

    Project check

    • ProjectsList a job’s joints and see which WPQR and WPS cover each one, and which welders hold a current qualification.
    • A drawing of every jointA clean drawing built from the values you enter, updated as you type.
    • PlannerTry one joint at a time and get the answer as you type; keep the good ones on a shortlist.
    • Project reportA PDF of what the job is covered for, on which WPQR and WPS, and by which welders.
    • Coverage mapEverything your WPQRs, or your welders, cover by thickness and diameter or position, with gaps and overlaps marked.

    WPS & WPQR

    • Joint drawings on the WPSThe joint preparation with editable dimensions and runs, printed on Atlas’s WPS form.
    • A WPS from a drawingStart a WPS from the joint itself, the other way round from importing one.
    • Edit a WPS togetherSee who else is working on a WPS and step in, without overwriting each other.
    • WPS form in your languageEnglish, Portuguese or Spanish, with an optional second language under every label.
    • Weld sequence on importThe welding sequence of a double-sided weld, picked up when a WPS is imported.
    • Faster WPQR entryCopy just the fields you choose from a similar WPQR, and move through the ranges from the keyboard.

    Qualifications

    • QR code on the welder passScan it with a phone to see that welder’s current qualifications.
    • Qualified welders, on a phoneWhich welders are qualified for a WPS, on a page made for a phone.
    • Operators and NDT inspectorsWelding operator (ISO 14732) and NDT inspector (ISO 9712) qualifications on the same board, with the same reminders.
  3. PlannedQ1 2027

    Move Into Elenchon When You Need the Floor

    When Elenchon launches, your Atlas library and qualifications move into it, for shops that want full shop-floor traceability on their own hardware. Atlas carries on for shops that only need procedures and welders in the cloud.
