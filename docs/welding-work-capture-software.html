Capture That Gets Out of the Worker's Way

Shop-floor weld data capture software is the app welders use to record work as it happens — which joint, which parameters, by whom, and when — so every weld is traceable back to the person and procedure behind it. Shop-floor data capture only works if the floor actually uses it. Elenchon's workflow app is built touch-first for the person holding the torch: reach the right joint or work step, enter the parameters, and move on — no training, no clutter, no desktop detour. Because capture is the moment traceability is either created or lost, every screen is engineered for speed under real conditions: gloves, bad lighting, and codes read off a label. It is the sovereign, on-premise alternative to cloud welding software.

10-Second Navigation

The fastest path from picking up a job to recording it — measured in seconds, not menus.

A streamlined, mobile-first interface gets operators to the right project step and inputting data in ten seconds or less, so capturing work never slows production down.

More Than Welding

Universal Work Types

Track every activity that touches the part — not just the welds.

  • Custom work templates: Define fit-up, cutting, assembly or any process once, save it as an organization-wide template, and capture it anywhere without extra setup.

Offline-First by Design

Offline-First Work Capture

Not "works if the Wi-Fi drops" — built to run disconnected, first-class.

Most software treats offline as a failure mode to survive. Elenchon treats it as the normal case: capture is built offline-first, so welders record work with no connection at all — and the data catches up on its own the moment they're back on the network. On a shop floor full of dead spots, that's the difference between a tool people trust and one they fight.

  • Disconnected is the default, not the exception: Start and submit a capture with zero connectivity — the app never waits on the network to let a welder work.
  • Durable on-device queue: Every offline entry is held safely on the device and syncs automatically, in order, the moment the connection returns.
  • Nothing lost in a dead zone: Welders keep working through a Wi-Fi blackspot without stopping to check whether the entry "went through."
  • Standard feature: Offline-first work capture is included on the Standard plan; the offline document viewer — saving a WPS or plan for instant access anywhere — stays available on every plan.
Audit Readiness

Every Entry Becomes Audit Evidence

Each capture is attributed, timestamped and version-pinned the moment it lands. That turns everyday floor activity into version-pinned compliance ledgers for codes such as ISO 3834, EN 1090 and ASME IX — so when the auditors arrive, the record of who did what, and when, is instant and undisputed.

On-Premise, Not Cloud

Your Floor Data Never Leaves the Shop

Most floor-capture apps are cloud apps: every entry your welders make is streamed to someone else's server, behind someone else's outages. Elenchon is the opposite — a containerized appliance installed in your facility, fully under your jurisdiction.

Shop-Floor Weld Capture, Answered

How Elenchon captures floor work fast, offline, and on your own hardware.

What is shop-floor weld data capture software?

It is the app welders use to record work as it happens — which joint, which parameters, by whom, and when — so every weld is traceable back to the person and procedure behind it. Elenchon runs that capture on-premise, keeping the record on hardware you own.

How fast is data entry on the floor?

The mobile-first interface is built to get an operator to the right project step and inputting data in ten seconds or less, using touch-and-PIN sign-in so entries are attributed without slowing anyone down.

Can it capture work when there is no Wi-Fi?

Yes — that's the design, not a workaround. Capture is offline-first: a welder can start and submit an entry with no connection at all. No. You can define custom work types — fit-up, cutting, assembly, or any process — save them as organization-wide templates, and capture any floor activity in the same traceable structure as your welds.

Is captured data stored on-premise or in the cloud?

On-premise. Elenchon is an appliance installed in your facility, so captured work never touches a shared cloud server. Authorised staff and auditors can still connect remotely through Phrakton's zero-trust, post-quantum-safe overlay.

How does this fit the wider platform?

Capture is the front door. Elenchon is full welding management software — the captured data flows into your project structure, the WPS/PQR/WPQ workflow, the weld traceability chain, and much more.
