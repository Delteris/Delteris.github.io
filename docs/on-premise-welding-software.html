Ownership

Absolute Data Ownership

Your records sit on your hardware — and stay yours, even if you leave.

Sovereignty means the data is physically and legally yours. Elenchon is built so you are never renting access to your own history.

  • On your physical hardware: Manufacturing data lives on a dedicated local server in your facility, under your jurisdiction — never on a shared cloud server.

Continuity

No Cloud to Depend On

An outage somewhere else can't stop production here.

When your traceability system lives in someone else's data center, their bad day becomes yours. Elenchon removes that dependency entirely.

  • Insulated from outages: A vendor or internet outage can never stop the floor from capturing work or lock you out of your own records.

Questions & Answers

On-Premise Welding Software, Answered

What sovereignty, offline capability and owned hardware actually mean for your shop.

What is on-premise welding software?

It is welding software installed on hardware inside your own facility, rather than accessed as a cloud service. Your weld records live on your server, under your control, and the system keeps working whether or not you have an internet connection. Elenchon is delivered exactly this way.

Is Elenchon cloud-based or on-premise?

On-premise. Elenchon is a containerized appliance installed in your facility — the sovereign alternative to cloud welding software. Your data never touches a shared server, there are no forced updates, and an internet outage cannot stop production.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The platform runs on your local network, and offline work capture queues entries on-device during a network drop and syncs them automatically on reconnect. The offline document viewer — saving a WPS or plan for instant access — is available on every plan.

How does on-premise affect cost?

It makes cost predictable. You pay one published, flat rate per appliance with a generous block of workers included and any extra seats at a low, published €9/month, the hardware is passed through at cost, and every add-on and fee is listed in plain euros — so your finance team can model total cost of ownership up front instead of watching an uncapped subscription grow. See the full on-premise vs. cloud comparison.

What happens to our data if we cancel?

It stays with you. The data is on your physical hardware, and you keep permanent view-only access to all historical records for audits. There is no lock-in and no exit fee.

Can staff still access it remotely?

Yes, securely. Authorised staff and auditors connect through Phrakton, a zero-trust, post-quantum-safe overlay, while the appliance opens no inbound ports to the public internet — remote reach without exposing your factory.
