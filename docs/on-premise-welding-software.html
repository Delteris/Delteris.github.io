Audit & Regulatory Readiness

Sovereignty Regulators Increasingly Expect

Data sovereignty is where regulation is heading. The EU's NIS2 directive, in force across member states in 2026, requires the controls Elenchon is built on — access control, encryption in transit and at rest, and regularly tested backups. It reaches fabrication shops two ways:

  • Through the supply chain — covered customers must vet their suppliers' security.
  • Through widening scope — every revision has expanded who it covers.

No open inbound ports, post-quantum-safe encryption and automated local backups put you ahead of that curve — alongside the ISO 3834, EN 1090 and ASME IX evidence Elenchon already produces. And an on-premise appliance adds no third-party processor or cross-border question.

Questions & Answers

On-Premise Welding Software, Answered

What sovereignty, offline capability and owned hardware actually mean for your shop.

Does it work offline?

Yes — offline work capture is a first-class mode, not a fallback. Authorised devices reach the appliance through Phrakton's encrypted fabric, on-site and off-site alike. When that connection drops, capture keeps going: entries are held in a durable on-device queue and sync automatically, in order, the moment the connection returns — nothing lost in a Wi-Fi dead zone. The offline document viewer — saving a WPS or plan for instant access — is available on every plan.

How does on-premise affect cost?

It makes cost predictable. You pay one published, flat rate per appliance with a generous block of workers included and any additional seats kept modest at €9/month, the hardware is passed through at cost, and every add-on and fee is listed in plain euros — so your finance team can model total cost of ownership up front instead of watching an uncapped subscription grow. See the full on-premise vs. cloud comparison.

What happens to our data if we cancel?

It stays with you. The data is on your physical hardware, and you keep view-only access to all historical records for audits. There is no lock-in and no exit fee.

Can staff still access it remotely?

Yes, securely. Authorised staff — and even auditors — connect through Phrakton, our secure network overlay, while the appliance stays off the public internet — remote reach without exposing your factory.

Isn't on-premise more to maintain?

Not the way we run it. Your only job is to keep the appliance powered on. We connect in remotely over the Phrakton fabric to apply updates and monitor it — your team never patches a thing — and resolve the large majority of issues without anyone leaving their desk. If something critical ever genuinely can't be fixed remotely, we come to your facility to fix it at no charge — anywhere in the world. You get on-premise ownership without an on-premise maintenance burden.

What is on-premise welding software?

It is welding software installed on hardware inside your own facility, rather than accessed as a cloud service. Your weld records live on your server, under your control, and the appliance runs on your own local network — no vendor cloud between you and your data. Elenchon is delivered exactly this way.

Does running it ourselves mean maintaining a server and updates?

No — on-premise here doesn't mean IT overhead. Elenchon ships as a self-contained containerized appliance we install and configure; there's no server for your team to administer, no update project to run, and nothing to patch on a schedule. You get the ownership of on-premise without inheriting the sysadmin work that usually comes with it.
