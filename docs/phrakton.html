Retire the VPN. Keep the Access.

A VPN puts a door in your perimeter and hands out keys: one compromised credential drops an attacker onto your entire network. Phrakton removes the door. What It Does

Any App. Any Server. One Secure Fabric.

Phrakton secures remote and on-site access to the applications you already run — on local servers, in a rack, or in the cloud — without exposing any of them.

Replace Your VPN

Per-application, per-identity access instead of network-wide tunnels. No VPN concentrator to patch, no exposed login portal to brute-force, no lateral movement to contain.

Clean Per-App URLs

Every internal application gets its own dedicated domain. Staff open a normal URL in a normal browser — no IP addresses, no port numbers, no client-side ceremony.

Works Behind Any Firewall

Phrakton connects outbound-only over standard TLS. No inbound firewall rules, no port forwarding, no DMZ — it runs from inside strict corporate networks without a single exception request for inbound traffic.

Post-Quantum-Safe by Default

Every tunnel uses post-quantum-hybrid key exchange, closing the door on “harvest now, decrypt later.” The traffic you protect today stays protected tomorrow.

Cryptographic Multitenancy

Each site or company operates in its own cryptographically isolated tenant. Access policies are deny-by-default: an identity reaches exactly the services it was granted, and nothing else.

LAN Fast, Remote Ready

On-site traffic stays on-site at LAN speed; remote sessions ride the encrypted fabric. The same URL works in both places — the overlay picks the best path automatically.

Under the Hood

Zero Trust, End to End

Phrakton is built on OpenZiti, the open-source zero-trust networking platform. Connections are brokered by cryptographic identity: a device proves who it is before any resource becomes reachable, and every hop is mutually authenticated and encrypted. The result is an overlay network your applications live on — and the public internet cannot see.

  • Identity, Not Location: A stolen password on an unknown device reaches nothing. Access follows verified cryptographic identities, never IPs.
  • Deny by Default: Every identity is granted an explicit list of services. There is no “inside the network” to be trusted from.
  • Operated for You: We run the fabric, monitor it with our own telemetry, and stand behind it with a written 99.9% uptime commitment and automatic invoice credits.

Questions & Answers

Zero-Trust Access, Answered

What IT teams ask before retiring their VPN.

Is Phrakton a VPN replacement?

Yes. A VPN drops a device onto your whole network; Phrakton grants access per application, per identity. No network-level access to move laterally through, no exposed concentrator to attack, and every session mutually authenticated.

Can I use Phrakton without Elenchon?

Yes. Phrakton is a standalone zero-trust access product for any company — it secures remote and LAN access to any application on your local servers or in the cloud. It also ships built into every Elenchon appliance.

Do we need firewall changes?

No inbound rules, ever. Phrakton connects outbound-only over standard TLS, so it works from inside corporate networks and behind strict firewalls — nothing listens on the public internet.

What does post-quantum-safe mean?

Tunnels use post-quantum-hybrid key exchange — classical and quantum-resistant cryptography combined. Traffic recorded today cannot be decrypted by a future quantum computer.
