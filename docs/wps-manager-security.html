A Full Audit Trail

Every Change, Attributed and Timestamped

An audit question becomes a lookup, not an investigation — because the app already recorded who did what, and when.

  • Everything is recorded: Every recompute, import, edit and verification is logged — what changed, who changed it, and when — and the log is searchable, so you answer an auditor's question in a lookup rather than a reconstruction.
  • Attributed to a person: Each entry carries the user behind it, so the register's history is a chain of accountable actions, not an anonymous set of edits.
  • Retention you control: Choose how long the activity log, register backups and old versions are kept — or keep them indefinitely.

Access You Control

Role-Based Access, Down to a Read-Only Seat

Give each person exactly the access their job needs — including a look-but-don't-touch seat for an outside inspector.

  • Admins, editors and viewers: Editors change data; viewers can only look. Grant each person the role their job calls for, and no more.
  • A seat for your inspector: Hand a read-only seat to your TÜV or notified-body inspector — full visibility, zero risk of an accidental change.
  • Rules you set: Choose how imports land and how your data is retained, so the workflow matches how your shop actually operates.

Isolation & Hardening

Your Tenancy, Kept to Itself

One tenant per instance, hardened by default — the same discipline as the on-premise Elenchon platform, applied to the cloud.

  • Single-tenant isolation: Your register and documents live in their own database, never pooled with another shop's — your data is yours alone.
  • Hardened by default: Signed sessions, a strong password policy, account lockout and per-IP rate limiting are on out of the box, not features you have to switch on.
  • Approval and retention are yours: You decide how imports land and how long logs, backups and versions are kept — the controls sit with you, not with us.

Where Your Data Lives

Hosted in the EU, Encrypted End to End

Your procedures stay in the jurisdiction you operate in, protected in transit and at rest.

  • EU-hosted: Your tenancy runs on infrastructure located in the European Union, so your procedures and register stay within the jurisdiction you operate in.
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest: Traffic to your tenancy is encrypted over TLS, and your data is encrypted where it's stored — in the database and in backups alike.
  • Backups you can rely on: Your register is versioned and backed up on a schedule you control, so a mistake is a rollback, not a loss.
  • Need full sovereignty? Shops that require the system of record entirely on hardware they own run the on-premise Elenchon platform instead — same team, same discipline.
