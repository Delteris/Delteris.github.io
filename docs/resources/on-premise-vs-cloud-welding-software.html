Managed On-Premise:
On-Premise Without the IT Burden.

"On-premise" still makes people think of server rooms, patch nights and VPNs. Managed on-premise keeps your data in your building and hands the rest back to the vendor.

By the Delteris team · Last reviewed 23 September 2026 · All guides

The Short Answer

Managed on-premise means the software runs on hardware in your building, but the vendor installs it, updates it and fixes it remotely. You keep the data ownership of on-premise without needing an IT department — your only job is to keep it powered on. It removes the old objections to on-premise. It isn't right for everyone: if you only need documents organised and findable, cloud is simpler.

What Changed

The Old Objections, Answered

  • "It becomes an IT project." A managed appliance arrives configured and is installed by the vendor. There is no server for your team to build or administer.
  • "We'd have to patch it." Updates and monitoring are applied remotely by the vendor.
  • "Remote access means open ports." Authorised staff reach the appliance through an encrypted overlay network, while it stays off the public internet. See how Elenchon does it on the security page.
  • "The on-premise edition is the cut-down one." On a managed appliance, the whole platform runs locally.
A Fair Answer

When Cloud Is the Better Fit

If the job is keeping your WPS library organised and your welders' qualifications visible, cloud is quicker: nothing to install, one login, running today. That is why we built Elenchon Atlas as a cloud product. On-premise earns its place when records are audit-critical, traceability runs down to the joint, or the data can't leave the building — the job of the Elenchon platform.

Before You Sign

Five Questions for Any On-Premise Vendor

  • Who installs it, and who fixes it when it breaks?
  • Who applies updates and security patches — and how often?
  • How does remote access work? Open ports and self-run VPNs are a warning sign.
  • What keeps working when the internet drops?
  • What happens to your data if you leave? Ask about exit fees and access to your history.

Questions & Answers

Managed On-Premise, Answered

Do I need IT staff for managed on-premise software?

No. The vendor installs, updates and repairs the appliance remotely. Your part is keeping it powered and connected.

Is on-premise more secure than cloud?

Not automatically. It reduces exposure when the appliance has no open ports and data never leaves the building — but the implementation matters more than the label. Ask how remote access and updates are secured.

Who owns the hardware?

That varies by vendor. With Elenchon, you buy the appliance at cost and it is yours, along with the data on it. See pricing and deployment.

What happens if I cancel?

With Elenchon there is no exit fee, and you keep view-only access to your historical records for audits. Ask any vendor for the same in writing.
