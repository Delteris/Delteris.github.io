Welder Qualification Validity:
Continuity, Expiry and Revalidation.

A welder's qualification doesn't stay valid on its own. Here is what keeps it alive under ISO 9606-1 and ASME Section IX — and what quietly ends it.

By the Delteris team · Last reviewed 23 September 2026 · All guides

The Short Answer

Under ISO 9606-1, a welder's qualification stays valid only while two things hold: every six months, someone responsible for welding confirms the welder has kept working within its range; and the qualification is revalidated on one of three routes — a retest every three years, two production welds tested every two years, or open-ended extension under an ISO 3834-2 or -3 quality system. Under ASME Section IX, a qualification for a process expires if the welder doesn't use that process for six months. Miss either and the certificate on file is no longer valid, whether or not anyone has noticed.

The Record

What a Welder Qualification Covers

A welder qualification — the WPQ, or welder certificate — proves that one person can make sound welds within a defined range: process, material group, product type, thickness, diameter and position. It qualifies the welder. The procedure has its own records — see pWPS vs WPQR vs WPS.

The Rules

Two Clocks, Two Codes

Continuity and validity run separately. Either one can end a qualification.

Welder qualification continuity and validity under ISO 9606-1 and ASME Section IX
Clock ISO 9606-1 ASME Section IX
Continuity Confirmed every 6 months that the welder has worked within the qualified range (clause 9.2). No confirmation, no valid certificate. The qualification for a process expires if the welder hasn't used it for 6 months (QW-322.1).
Validity One route, chosen at issue (clause 9.3): (a) retest every 3 years; (b) every 2 years, two production welds from the last 6 months tested by RT, UT or destructive tests; (c) no end date while the welder works for the same manufacturer under ISO 3834-2 or -3, with documented weld quality. No fixed end date while continuity holds.
Once lapsed Requalification by test. Renewal by a single test coupon, which restores the welder's previous qualifications for that process (QW-322.2).

Both codes also let a qualification be revoked when there is a specific reason to question the welder's skill. ISO 9606-1 is under revision — check which edition your contracts and certificates cite.

Why It Slips

Easy Rules, Hard Bookkeeping

  • It multiplies. Twenty welders, three processes each, two clocks per qualification — over a hundred dates, all moving.
  • It runs per process. A welder who stops TIG for seven months loses TIG while their MAG qualification stays valid.
  • Lapses are silent. An expired certificate looks the same in a folder as a valid one. The gap shows up at audit, or after a weld has been made.
  • Evidence lives elsewhere. The proof that a welder kept working in range sits in production records — weld logs that are rarely filed next to the certificate.
Keeping Track

What Good Tracking Looks Like

  • Both clocks per qualification, not a single expiry date.
  • A roster you read at a glance — who is valid, who is due, who has lapsed.
  • Warnings before the date, on screen and by email, so the confirmation or retest happens in time.
  • A signed, dated record of every confirmation and requalification, with the certificate attached.

A spreadsheet can do this with enough discipline; software makes it hard to miss. Either way, the tool records and warns — whether a welder is qualified stays a judgement for a qualified person.

Questions & Answers

Welder Qualification Validity, Answered

How long is an ISO 9606-1 welder certificate valid?

It depends on the revalidation route chosen when it was issued: three years with a retest, two years renewable with tested production welds, or open-ended under an ISO 3834-2/-3 quality system. On every route, the six-month confirmation must also be kept up.

Who confirms the six-month continuity?

The person responsible for welding activities at the employer — typically the welding coordinator — or the examiner or examining body. It is a documented confirmation, not a retest.

Does an ASME welder qualification expire?

Only through non-use: if the welder doesn't weld with a process for six months, the qualification for that process expires (QW-322.1). It can be renewed with a single test coupon (QW-322.2).

Can software decide whether a welder is qualified?

It shouldn't. Good software keeps the records, runs the clocks and warns you in time. Whether a welder is qualified for a job is a judgement for a qualified person, who signs it.
