Delivered Ahead of Plan

Offline-First Capture, Already In

Full offline-first data capture was originally scheduled for month six. It ships with the platform at deployment instead — the floor keeps recording with no connection at all, and every entry syncs automatically on reconnect.

September · Deployment & Rollout

The Platform Goes Live on Your Shop Floor

We install and configure the local server on site, then set the system up alongside your team rather than in place of it.

  • Built with you, not for you: Your welding coordinator and office staff enter the first WPS records, qualifications, work types and project structures with us at their shoulder — so by the time we leave, the people who will run the system day to day have already done every operation themselves once.
  • Trained by role: Floor staff are trained on capture; office staff on management, qualifications and document generation.
  • Owned from day one: The baseline for compliance tracking is established, and owned internally, before anyone is asked to rely on it.

Month 6 · Major Feature Delivery

The Next Substantial Block of Functionality

The next major version. Standard-tier features arrive at no additional cost; Premium is an optional upgrade, priced openly at launch.

Month 6

Granular NDT Tracking Standard

Deep, integrated Non-Destructive Testing tracking, delivered natively to all Standard users — test requests, results, and their link to the specific joint and repair cycle. Included at your existing monthly rate, with no upgrade charge.

Month 6

PWHT Cycle Tracking Standard

Post-weld heat treatment tracked by total number of cycles and total cycle time, tied to the joints it applies to — the downstream record a code auditor expects alongside the weld.

Month 6

Productivity Manager Premium

The measured side of the same picture — throughput per worker, work type and project stage, time spent per operation, and historical trends you can put in front of a client or use to quote the next job.

Month 6

Visual Production Tracker Premium

A live, visual read on the state of production across the whole project — which pieces are being worked right now, how much activity each part of the structure is carrying, and where work is accumulating or stalling. Bottlenecks become visible while there is still time to act on them, rather than surfacing in a report at the end of the month.

All plan, add-on, deployment and hardware prices are published in full on our pricing page — including the Premium tier once its feature set is finalized. Everything listed under the Standard tier is included at your existing monthly rate, with no upgrade charge.
