Product Roadmap

And Always Moving Forward.

Q1 2027 · Launch

The Platform Goes Live on Your Floor

Your team runs compliance tracking on real work, owned internally from day one — with offline-first capture included, ahead of its original month-six plan. Full detail on the deployment page.

Months 1–5 · Refinement in Real Use

The Product Bends to How You Actually Work

When your feedback shapes Elenchon most: the floor app stays fast in real conditions, features adjust to how your team works, and new templates and export formats arrive as your paperwork needs them.

Month 6 · V2 Release

Two Outcomes, One Release

The next major version delivers two things the floor has asked for. Standard-tier features arrive at no extra cost; Premium is an optional upgrade, priced openly at launch.

V2 · Standard

Close the Compliance Loop Downstream of the Weld

The records a code auditor expects after the arc stops, tied natively to the joint — no separate binder, no extra charge.

  • Granular NDT tracking: test requests, results, and their link to the specific joint and repair cycle.
  • PWHT cycle tracking: post-weld heat treatment by total cycles and total cycle time, tied to the joints it covers.
V2 · Premium

See and Measure Your Production

Turn the data the floor already captures into a live read on output and where work is stalling — evidence you can put in front of a client or use to quote the next job.

  • Productivity Manager: throughput per worker, work type and project stage, with historical trends.
  • Visual Production Tracker: a live map of the project showing what's moving now and where bottlenecks form, while there's still time to act.

All plan, add-on, deployment and hardware prices are published in full on our pricing page — including Premium once its feature set is finalized.

Elenchon Atlas · Next Release

Atlas 1.3: Can We Weld This Job?

The next Atlas release, in development now and included for every Atlas customer at no extra cost. At its centre is Project check: the joints a job needs, checked against the WPQRs and welder qualifications you already keep in Atlas. We’ll give a date once it’s close.

Atlas 1.3 · Project check

Know What a Job Needs Before It Starts

  • Projects: list the joints a job needs (plate or pipe, thickness, position, processes) and see for each one which WPQR and WPS cover it and which welders hold a current qualification, as entered.
  • A drawing of every joint: a clean drawing built from the values you enter, updated as you type.
  • Planner: try one joint at a time and get the answer as you type; keep the ones worth keeping on a shortlist.
  • Project report: a PDF of what the job is covered for, on which WPQR and WPS, and by which welders, in English, Portuguese or Spanish.
  • Coverage map: everything your WPQRs, or your welders, cover by thickness and diameter or position, with the gaps and overlaps marked.
Atlas 1.3 · WPS & WPQR

Draw, Write and Print WPSs

  • Joint drawings on the WPS: the joint preparation drawn with editable dimensions and runs, printed on Atlas’s WPS form.
  • A WPS from a drawing: start a WPS from the joint itself, the other way round from importing one.
  • Edit a WPS together: see who else is working on a WPS and step in, without overwriting each other’s changes.
  • WPS form in your language: Atlas’s own WPS form in English, Portuguese or Spanish, with a second language under every label when a foreign client or inspector reads it.
  • Weld sequence on import: the welding sequence of a double-sided weld picked up when a WPS is imported.
  • Faster WPQR entry: copy just the fields you choose from a similar WPQR, and move through the ranges from the keyboard.
Atlas 1.3 · Qualifications

Check a Qualification at the Joint

  • QR code on the welder pass: scan it with a phone to see that welder’s current qualifications.
  • Qualified welders, on a phone: see which welders are qualified for a WPS, on a page made for a phone.
  • Operators and NDT inspectors: welding operator (ISO 14732) and NDT inspector (ISO 9712) qualifications on the same board, with the same reminders.
