The Organized Home for Every WPS You Have

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place — so you always know exactly which procedures you hold and can find any one of them instantly. Elenchon WPS Manager is a cloud tool built around one idea: authoring a WPS was never the hard part — keeping hundreds of them organized, and getting your existing ones into a system without weeks of manual entry, is. Our parser reads your existing WPS files and extracts every field, so your whole library becomes filterable from day one. It is the WPS library from the Elenchon platform, offered standalone in the cloud.

Parser-Driven Import

Get Your WPSs In — In Minutes, Not Weeks

Upload the WPS documents you already have. The parser does the data entry for you.

Most WPS tools make you re-type every procedure into their format, or wrestle a fragile Excel template — turning a folder of existing WPSs into days or weeks of work. Elenchon WPS Manager reads your documents instead.

  • Reads your real WPS files: The parser reconstructs each WPS form and reads its cells, pulling process, materials, positions, ranges and more into structured fields — automatically.
  • Batch import: Bring in a whole stack of existing WPSs at once, not one form at a time.
  • No weird spreadsheets: No fragile Excel intermediary, no multi-day data-entry project to get started. Upload, and your library is populated.
  • Built to fit your template: The parser is tuned to your company's own WPS layout during onboarding, so it reads your paperwork — not a generic form you'd have to conform to.
Search & Filter

Find Any Procedure in Seconds

Because every field is extracted, you filter by what actually matters — not filenames.

A folder of PDFs is a haystack. A WPS library with every field extracted is a database you can question. Ask it for exactly the procedure you need.

  • Filter by real criteria: Narrow your library by welding process, base or filler material, position, thickness range and more — down to a specific combination of processes, with or without a backing pass, in a couple of clicks.
  • Field-based, not filename-based: No more scrolling filenames or opening documents to check what's inside — the values are indexed and searchable.
  • Always know what you hold: One central library means you always know the full set of procedures your shop has qualified — nothing lost in someone's inbox or a shared drive.
The Whole Linked Set

Every Record Behind a WPS, Attached and Visible

See the WPQR that qualifies each WPS, and the pWPS behind that WPQR — the full document chain, linked in one place.

A WPS never stands alone: it's qualified by a WPQR, which in turn traces back to a preliminary WPS (pWPS). Elenchon WPS Manager keeps that whole chain attached, so from any WPS you can see exactly which records support it — no hunting through folders.

  • WPQR attached to the WPS: Open any WPS and see the Procedure Qualification Record that qualifies it, attached and displayed in a clean, readable layout.
  • pWPS attached to the WPQR: Trace each WPQR back to the preliminary WPS behind it — the full document chain, pWPS to WPQR to WPS, linked and visible.
  • Attach any supporting file: Keep the related documents together with the procedure they belong to, so the paperwork trail is always one click away, not scattered across drives.
  • Print to PDF: Produce a tidy PDF of a record for easy viewing and transferring to a customer, inspector, or colleague.

Note: WPS Manager attaches, links and displays these records — capturing qualification data live on the shop floor, and deriving qualified ranges from it, is the job of the full Elenchon platform.

Questions & Answers

WPS Management Software, Answered

What Elenchon WPS Manager does, how import works, and where it fits.

What is WPS management software?

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place, so you always know which procedures your shop holds and can find any one instantly. Elenchon WPS Manager is a cloud tool that imports your existing WPS documents, extracts every field, and lets you filter your whole library by process, material and more.

How does the WPS import / parser work?

You upload your existing WPS documents and the parser reconstructs each form and reads its cells, pulling process, materials, ranges and other values into structured fields automatically. During onboarding we tune the parser to your company's own WPS template, so it reads your paperwork — turning what would be weeks of spreadsheet data entry into a quick import.

Can I filter or search my WPSs by welding process?

Yes. Because every field is extracted at import, you filter your library by real criteria — welding process, base and filler material, position, thickness range and more. You can narrow it down to a single process, or a specific combination of processes with or without a backing pass, in a couple of clicks, instead of scrolling filenames.

Does WPS Manager create WPSs, or just store them?

Its core job today is management — import, organize, filter, and keep supporting PQRs alongside. A spreadsheet-style builder for creating and bulk-editing WPSs in the cloud is on the roadmap. It deliberately does not derive qualified ranges from test data — that metallurgy is handled by the full Elenchon qualification platform.

How much does Elenchon WPS Manager cost?

It includes 50 GB of storage (tens of thousands of WPSs); extra 50 GB blocks are €10/month if you ever need them. One tenancy is one system — we recommend one instance per isolated factory but don't police it. Onboarding and parser setup for your template are included and handled directly by the founders.

How is it different from the Elenchon platform?

Elenchon WPS Manager is the cloud WPS library on its own — for shops that just need their procedures organized and searchable. The full Elenchon platform adds shop-floor WPQR capture, range qualification, welder continuity (WPQ) and full traceability on an on-premise appliance. Same team; WPS Manager is the lightweight cloud slice, and it's the natural starting point if you later grow into the full platform.
