Welder Qualification

Your Welders' Qualifications, Tracked in the Same Place

The same system keeps every welder's qualifications current — who's qualified for what, and which lapse is coming. See welder qualification management →

01

A Grid That Shows Status at a Glance

Welders down the rows, the processes they hold across the columns — each cell coloured by that process's soonest-due qualification, green through red. Filter by welder, process, standard or anything recorded inside a qualification.

02

Two Clocks, Watched Separately

Expiry and continuity tracked as separate dates, each warned ahead — a month out, two weeks, the week of.

03

Requalification, Signed and Dated

Renew continuity behind a password signature — an immutable, attributed stamp on the record. Atlas keeps the attestation; it doesn't judge validity for you.

04

The Certificate, Kept With the Record

Optionally attach the qualification certificate — scan, PDF or image — and view it on the record. Evidence you can pull up in seconds.

05

Nothing Overwritten

Every qualification a welder has ever held stays on file. Requalify or supersede; the past record is never lost.

06

Warned Before It Lapses

Due dates show on your dashboard by default — and switch on email reminders to get a heads-up ahead of each deadline, so a requalification never sneaks up on you.

Questions & Answers

WPS Management Software, Answered

What Atlas does, what it costs, and where it fits.

What is WPS management software?
WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organised, searchable and in one place. Atlas imports the WPS you already hold — Excel or PDF — extracts every field into structured records, proposes reviewed edits, and exports the register and its linked files on demand. Full guide: pWPS vs WPQR vs WPS.

We already keep our WPS in a spreadsheet. Why change?
A hand-maintained spreadsheet is stale the moment a WPS is revised, and nobody can say what it holds without scrolling it. Atlas reads every procedure into structured data and patches only the cells you approve, so your workbook survives — searchable, filterable by what’s inside each form, and versioned with rollback.

What do I need to bring to get started?
The WPS you already hold, in Excel or PDF — the original files, not scans. No template to fill in and nothing to re-key: onboarding starts with a few real documents, so the parser is proven on your form before you commit. Your register stays your file.

Does Atlas create WPS, or just manage them?
Management: import, review, organise, filter, version and export, with WPQRs and pWPS alongside. Each WPS exports as a clean Excel workbook — the whole register too — and re-imports losslessly. A cloud builder for authoring is on the roadmap; shop-floor WPQR capture belongs to the Elenchon platform.

How much does it cost, and is it per user?
€80 per month, or €720 a year — an effective €60 — billed annually. Flat and published, never per seat: your whole team works in one tenancy with admin, editor and read-only roles. 50 GB of storage is included; extra 50 GB blocks are €10/month. Onboarding and parser setup are included.

What support do I get at this price?

Direct support from the founders, included — no ticket queue, no paid tier. You email the people who built the app and hear back within a business day. Because the same people handle onboarding, a request that needs a parser tweak goes straight to whoever can make it. And it's in writing: if an issue you report goes 3 business days without a reply from our team, that month is half price; after 5, it's free — credited to your next invoice, or added to your term on the annual plan.

Does Atlas track welder qualifications (WPQ)?
Yes. Each welder has a record with expiry and continuity tracked as separate dates and warned ahead, a colour-coded roster, signed-and-dated requalification, retained history and an optional certificate. Due dates appear on the dashboard, with email reminders you can switch on. Atlas records and warns; judging validity stays with you.

How is it different from the Elenchon platform?
Atlas is the cloud register on its own — procedures and qualifications organised, current and searchable. The full Elenchon platform adds shop-floor WPQR capture, continuity derived from logged work and full traceability on an on-premise appliance. Same team, and Atlas is the natural starting point.
