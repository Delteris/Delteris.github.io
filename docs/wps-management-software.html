Where Your Welding Procedures Actually Live

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place — so you always know exactly which procedures you hold, every one linked to the records that qualify it. For decades a shop's procedures have lived in one enormous spreadsheet, maintained cell by cell, by hand — slow, error-prone, and out of date the moment a WPS is revised. Elenchon WPS Manager is what comes next: a system that reads your procedures. Import the WPSs you already have — Excel or PDF — and it extracts every field into a structured record you can search, filter, compare, correct and link, with revisions grouped and each WPS tied to its WPQR and pWPS. You manage the whole library here, in the app, not in a workbook you nurse along. The master register your auditors and head office expect is no longer the thing you maintain — it's a clean export the app produces on demand, complete with a folder of the linked WPS files. It is the WPS engine from the Elenchon platform, offered standalone in the cloud.

What It Does

From a Folder of WPSs to a Register That Runs Itself

Import, review, hold, search, verify and export — the full loop your register needs, one capability at a time.

01

Import — Excel & PDF, Both Native

Upload the WPS documents you already have, in the format you already have them. Two purpose-built parsers do the data entry — no re-typing every procedure into someone else's template.

  • Excel and PDF, both native: One parser reads Excel WPS workbooks; another reconstructs a PDF form's ruled grid and reads its cells. Process, materials, positions, ranges, pass-by-pass parameters and more land as structured fields, automatically.
  • Bilingual out of the box: The built-in profile understands English and Portuguese WPS layouts as shipped, so a mixed EN/PT form imports without special setup.
  • Batch import: Bring in a whole stack of existing WPSs at once, not one form at a time.
  • Heat input filled where it's blank: If a form leaves the heat-input column empty, the app computes it from the arc parameters (current × voltage × 60 ÷ travel speed × process efficiency, per EN 1011-1) and flags it for you to confirm against the PQR — never a silent number.
  • Tuned to your template: Standard ISO 15609-style forms work immediately; during onboarding we tune the parser to your company's exact layout, so it reads your paperwork — not a generic form you'd have to conform to.

02

Human-Reviewed Edits

Nothing touches your register until you approve it. Every import proposes a change-set — current value, new value, cell by cell — and you decide what lands.

  • See exactly what would change: The review queue lays out each proposed edit as current value → new value, down to the individual cell. Tick a cell to apply it, untick to leave it as it was.
  • Correct before you commit: Fix any value inline, or add a row for a field the parser missed — then approve. What lands is what you signed off on.
  • Low-confidence reads are flagged: Any cell the parser wasn't sure of — or a heat input it calculated — is shaded amber and surfaced for a check, so your attention goes exactly where it's needed.
  • Set your own approval bar: Choose how imports land — review every value, review only the uncertain ones (the default), or auto-apply everything and lean on versioning to undo.
  • Drift detection: If a WPS is edited after its values already landed, the app tells you the register is out of sync with the library and offers to re-sync — so the sheet never quietly drifts from the documents behind it.
  • Only approved cells are patched: Your workbook is never rebuilt from scratch. The app writes the confirmed cells into your own file and leaves everything else exactly as you had it.

03

Your Register, Visualised & Versioned

The register you keep is your workbook — not a model the app regenerates and hands back changed. WPS Manager holds the latest copy, shows it as a readable grid, and keeps a full history behind it.

  • Visualised, not just stored: Your master sheet opens as a fast, filterable grid — no more scrolling a giant spreadsheet or opening it in Excel just to look something up.
  • Every version kept: Each approved change-set produces a new register version (v1 → v2 → v3…). Roll back to any earlier snapshot; the current and newest versions are always protected.
  • Export on demand: Produce the updated register as a clean Excel workbook whenever you need it — plus a folder of the linked WPS files, zipped together, so the paperwork travels with the sheet.
  • Retention on your terms: Decide how long old versions and the activity trail are kept, or keep them indefinitely — an admin sets the policy per deployment.
04

A Library That Understands Your WPSs

Behind every register row is a fully-read document you can open, verify against its source, edit in place, and connect to the records that qualify it. Every WPS, pWPS and WPQR, parsed into structured records you can search, compare, correct and link.

  • Three linked document types, one home: WPS, preliminary WPS (pWPS) and WPQR each get their own tab, each imported the same way — drop an Excel or PDF, or add a WPQR record by hand. Every document sits alongside the procedures it relates to.
  • Revisions, grouped automatically: Several revisions of the same WPS number collapse into a single row with an “N revisions” chip and a switcher to move between them — newest first. On import, each incoming file is classified as a brand-new WPS, a fresh revision, or a correction to an existing one.
  • Edit and verify in place: Correct any field's value — or even its label — and save; the label re-titles that column in the register. A one-click “Verify” clears a low-confidence flag once you've checked it, and computed heat input gets its own verify so the amber clears across every pass at once.
  • The real detail, not just a filename: Header fields, the full pass table with each pass's side (inside / outside / both) and role (root / fill / cap / backing), base-material parts, filler and consumable, shielding and flux — all extracted, all editable, with a row you can add if the parser ever misses one.
  • Filter by what's actually inside: Narrow by welding process, base or filler material, position, thickness range and more — down to a specific combination of processes, with or without a backing pass, in a couple of clicks.
  • Facets that read the pass table: Filter on the values inside the passes themselves — side, role, process, polarity — as set-membership filters, so “every WPS that runs a 121 root” is one click, not a manual read.
  • Smart linking & the whole chain: Link a WPS to the WPQR that qualifies it, a WPQR to the several WPS it supports, a pWPS to the number it becomes — then see the related documents together and open any of them in a click.

05

Preview — Every Read Against the Original

The feature that makes a parser safe to rely on. Open any WPS in Preview and the app splits the screen: on the left, every field it read; on the right, your real file — the PDF form rendered in place, or the Excel sheet drawn inline.

  • The genuine source, not a re-render: A PDF opens as the real PDF, page and all, with its own zoom and fit-to-width; an Excel WPS renders as the actual sheet. You're checking against the document your welding engineer signed, not the app's interpretation of it.
  • Built to work in, not glance at: Preview is a verification workspace — it stays open while you work through a document and won't vanish on a stray click outside it. Widen the source pane with a draggable divider when a form is dense, up to half the screen.
  • Correct without leaving: Spot a misread? Edit the value — or its label — right inside Preview, tick a low-confidence field as verified, accept a computed heat input, and save. Compare and fix are the same view.
  • Everything stays in sync: A fix in Preview updates the library detail, the register column titles, and the review badge together — one correction, reflected everywhere it should be.

06

The Whole Linked Set

A WPS never stands alone: it's qualified by a WPQR, which in turn traces back to a preliminary WPS (pWPS). WPS Manager keeps that whole chain attached, so from any WPS you can see exactly which records support it.

  • WPQR attached to the WPS: Open any WPS and see the Procedure Qualification Record that qualifies it, attached and displayed in a clean, readable layout.
  • pWPS attached to the WPQR: Trace each WPQR back to the preliminary WPS behind it — the full document chain, pWPS to WPQR to WPS, linked and visible.
  • Attach any supporting file: Keep the related documents together with the procedure they belong to, so the paperwork trail is always one click away, not scattered across drives.
  • Where WPS Manager stops: It attaches, links and displays these records — capturing WPQR results live on the shop floor and surfacing them against the procedures they support is the job of the full Elenchon platform.
07

Heat Input Helper

A live scratch pad for the one calculation welders get wrong under pressure. Heat input is where a perfectly good procedure quietly goes out of qualification — the built-in helper makes the safe window impossible to miss.

  • Set the qualified range, then explore: Enter the heat-input range your PQR qualified for each weld region — root, fill, cap — then drag the current, voltage and travel-speed sliders to see the resulting heat input computed live.
  • Sliders that can't leave the window: The controls clamp to your PQR-qualified range, so you physically can't dial in a combination that falls outside what the procedure allows.
  • Multi-arc aware: Tandem and twin-arc setups get a lead and a trail head with their own current and voltage; the total heat input is summed across heads, the way the standard expects.
  • Pull straight from a WPS: Load a parsed WPS from your library and its per-region ranges and processes fill in automatically. It's a scratch pad; nothing here is saved or written to any sheet.

Questions & Answers

WPS Management Software, Answered

What Elenchon WPS Manager does, how import works, and where it fits.

What is WPS management software?

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place, so you always know which procedures your shop holds and can find any one instantly. Elenchon WPS Manager works from the master register most shops already keep: it imports your existing WPS documents, extracts every field, proposes reviewed edits to your register, and exports the updated workbook and its linked files whenever you need them.

Can it import Excel and PDF WPSs?

Yes — both, natively. One parser reads Excel WPS workbooks; another reconstructs a PDF form's grid and reads its cells. Either way, process, materials, ranges and pass-by-pass parameters land as structured fields, and the built-in profile reads English and Portuguese forms out of the box. Where a form leaves heat input blank, the app calculates it from the arc parameters and flags it for you to confirm.

Does an import change my register automatically?

Only if you choose to let it. By default every import queues as a proposed change-set: you see the current value beside the new value, cell by cell, and approve what lands — with low-confidence reads shaded for a closer look. You can set the bar higher (review every value) or lower (auto-apply and rely on versioning to undo). Only the cells you approve are patched into your own workbook; nothing is rebuilt.

Can I filter or search my WPSs by welding process?

Yes. Because every field is extracted at import, you filter your library by real criteria — welding process, base and filler material, position, thickness range and more. You can narrow it down to a single process, or a specific combination of processes with or without a backing pass, in a couple of clicks, instead of scrolling filenames.

Does WPS Manager create WPSs, or just manage them?

Today its job is management — import, review, organize, filter, version, and export, with supporting WPQRs and pWPSs kept alongside. It already exports a clean Excel workbook per WPS (or the whole register), and that file re-imports losslessly. A spreadsheet-style builder for writing and bulk-editing WPSs in the cloud is on the roadmap. It deliberately does not capture WPQR results on the floor — that shop-floor qualification capture is handled by the full Elenchon qualification platform.

How much does Elenchon WPS Manager cost?

€80 per month, or €720 per year (an effective €60 per month) billed annually. One flat, fully published price — no per-seat charges and no "contact us for a quote." It includes 50 GB of storage (tens of thousands of WPSs); extra 50 GB blocks are €10/month if you ever need them. One tenancy is one system — we recommend one instance per isolated factory but don't police it. Onboarding and parser setup for your template are included and handled directly by the founders.

How is it different from the Elenchon platform?

Elenchon WPS Manager is the cloud WPS register on its own — for shops that just need their procedures organized, current and searchable. The full Elenchon platform adds shop-floor WPQR capture, welder continuity (WPQ) and full traceability on an on-premise appliance. Same team; WPS Manager is the lightweight cloud slice, and it's the natural starting point if you later grow into the full platform.
