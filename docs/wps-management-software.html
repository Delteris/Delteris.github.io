Where Your Welding Procedures Actually Live

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place — so you always know which procedures you hold, each linked to the records that qualify it. For decades that's meant one giant spreadsheet, maintained cell by cell and stale the moment a WPS is revised. Atlas reads your procedures instead. Import the WPS you already have — Excel or PDF — and it extracts every field into a record you can search, filter, compare and link, with revisions grouped and each WPS tied to its WPQR. You manage the library in the app, not in a workbook you nurse along. Need the master register? It's a clean export, on demand, with the linked files alongside. It's the cloud side of Elenchon — and now it manages your welders' qualifications too.

Questions & Answers

WPS Management Software, Answered

What Atlas does, what it costs, and where it fits.

What is WPS management software?

It keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place, so you always know which procedures you hold and can find any one instantly. Atlas works from the register most shops already keep: it imports your existing WPS, extracts every field, proposes reviewed edits, and exports the updated workbook and its linked files whenever you need them.

We already keep our WPS in a spreadsheet. Why change?

A hand-maintained spreadsheet goes stale the moment a WPS is revised, and nobody can tell you what it holds without scrolling it. Atlas keeps your workbook — it patches the cells you approve rather than replacing your file — but reads every procedure into structured data behind it, so the register is searchable, filterable by what's inside each form, versioned with rollback, and exportable on demand. You stop maintaining the sheet and start querying it.

What do I need to bring to get started?

Just the WPS you already have, in Excel or PDF. There's no template to fill in and no data to re-key — onboarding starts with you sending a few real documents so we can prove the parser reads your form before you commit. Your existing register stays your file; the app works from it rather than replacing it.

Does Atlas create WPS, or just manage them?

Today its job is management — import, review, organize, filter, version and export, with WPQRs and pWPS alongside. It exports a clean Excel workbook per WPS (or the whole register), and that file re-imports losslessly. A cloud builder for writing and bulk-editing WPS is on the roadmap. It deliberately doesn't capture WPQR results on the floor — that's the full Elenchon platform's job.

How much does it cost, and is it per user?

€80 per month, or €720 per year — an effective €60 per month — billed annually. One flat, published price, never per seat and never a "contact us for a quote": your whole team works in one tenancy, with admin, editor and read-only roles, for the same price. It includes 50 GB of storage; extra 50 GB blocks are €10/month if you ever need them. Onboarding and parser setup are included.

What support do I get at this price?

Direct support from the founders, included — no ticket queue, no paid tier. You email the people who built the app and hear back within a business day. Because the same people handle onboarding, a request that needs a parser tweak goes straight to whoever can make it.

Does Atlas track welder qualifications (WPQ)?

Yes. Alongside your procedures, Atlas keeps a record for each welder — expiry and continuity tracked as two separate dates and warned ahead, a colour-coded roster, signed-and-dated requalification, retained history, and an optional certificate on the record. Due dates show on your dashboard, with email reminders you can switch on. It records and warns; it doesn't judge whether a qualification is valid. Shop-floor WPQR capture and continuity from logged work stay in the Elenchon platform.

How is it different from the Elenchon platform?

Atlas is the cloud register on its own — for shops that just need procedures and qualifications organized, current and searchable. The full Elenchon platform adds shop-floor WPQR capture, continuity derived from logged work, and full traceability on an on-premise appliance. Same team; Atlas is the cloud side, and the natural starting point if you later grow into the platform.
