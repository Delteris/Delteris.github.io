Where Your Welding Procedures Actually Live

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place — so you always know exactly which procedures you hold, every one linked to the records that qualify it. For decades a shop's procedures have lived in one enormous spreadsheet, maintained cell by cell, by hand — slow, error-prone, and out of date the moment a WPS is revised. Elenchon WPS Manager is what comes next: a system that reads your procedures. Import the WPSs you already have — Excel or PDF — and it extracts every field into a structured record you can search, filter, compare, correct and link, with revisions grouped and each WPS tied to the WPQR that qualifies it. You manage the whole library here, in the app, not in a workbook you nurse along. The master register your auditors and head office expect is no longer the thing you maintain — it's a clean export the app produces on demand, complete with a folder of the linked WPS files. It's the WPS slice of Elenchon, delivered as a cloud product of its own.

Questions & Answers

WPS Management Software, Answered

What Elenchon WPS Manager does, what it costs, and where it fits.

What is WPS management software?

WPS management software keeps your Welding Procedure Specifications organized, searchable and in one place, so you always know which procedures your shop holds and can find any one instantly. Elenchon WPS Manager works from the master register most shops already keep: it imports your existing WPS documents, extracts every field, proposes reviewed edits to your register, and exports the updated workbook and its linked files whenever you need them.

We already keep our WPSs in a spreadsheet. Why change?

Because a hand-maintained spreadsheet goes stale the moment a WPS is revised, and nobody can tell you what it holds without scrolling it. WPS Manager keeps your workbook — it patches the cells you approve rather than replacing your file — but reads every procedure into structured data behind it, so the register is searchable, filterable by what's actually inside each form, versioned with rollback, and exportable on demand. You stop maintaining the sheet by hand and start querying it.

What do I need to bring to get started?

Just the WPSs you already have, in Excel or PDF. There's no template to fill in and no data to re-key — onboarding begins with you sending a few real documents so we can prove the parser reads your exact form before you commit. Your existing register, if you keep one, stays your file; the app works from it rather than replacing it.

Does WPS Manager create WPSs, or just manage them?

Today its job is management — import, review, organize, filter, version, and export, with supporting WPQRs and pWPSs kept alongside. It already exports a clean Excel workbook per WPS (or the whole register), and that file re-imports losslessly. A spreadsheet-style builder for writing and bulk-editing WPSs in the cloud is on the roadmap. It deliberately does not capture WPQR results on the floor — that shop-floor qualification capture is handled by the full Elenchon qualification platform.

How much does it cost, and is it charged per user?

€90 per month, or €840 per year — an effective €70 per month — billed annually. One flat, fully published price, not per seat and never a "contact us for a quote": your whole team works in one tenancy, with admin, editor and read-only roles, for the same price. It includes 50 GB of storage (tens of thousands of WPSs); extra 50 GB blocks are €10/month if you ever need them. Onboarding and parser setup for your template are included, handled directly by the founders.

What kind of support do I get at this price?

Direct support from the founders, included — no ticket queue and no paid support tier. You email the people who built the app and hear back within one business day, usually sooner. Because the same people handle your onboarding, a support request that needs a parser tweak for your form goes straight to whoever can make it.

How is it different from the Elenchon platform?

Elenchon WPS Manager is the cloud WPS register on its own — for shops that just need their procedures organized, current and searchable. The full Elenchon platform adds shop-floor WPQR capture, welder continuity (WPQ) and full traceability on an on-premise appliance. Same team; WPS Manager is the WPS slice of Elenchon delivered as a cloud product, and it's the natural starting point if you later grow into the full platform.
