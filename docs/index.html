What fabrication shops ask before moving weld traceability and welder qualification onto their own hardware.

What is welding management software?

Welding management software digitizes a fabrication shop's welding records — welder qualifications (WPS, PQR, WPQ), weld tracking and traceability, procedure control and inspection history — so every joint is traceable and audit-ready. Elenchon does this entirely on-premise, keeping the full weld record on hardware you own.

Explore the platform →

Is Elenchon cloud-based or on-premise?

Elenchon is a 100% on-premise appliance installed in your facility — the sovereign alternative to cloud welding software (see the honest comparison). Your manufacturing data never touches a shared server, there are no forced updates, and you don't depend on a vendor's cloud to reach your own records.

How data sovereignty works →

Is Elenchon just weld traceability, or does it run qualification too?

Both, in one system — and that's the point. Most tools solve a single slice of a workshop, so you end up running twenty of them and stitching the gaps yourself. Elenchon is built to manage your whole floor from a welding perspective: alongside traceability it runs the full WPS/PQR/WPQ workflow, with live WPQ capture, automatic 6-month welder continuity from real floor entries, and expiry alerts — so you never miss a requalification or let a welder work outside an approved range, all from one partner rather than a stack of point products.

Every qualification, variable and process is verified and attributed the moment it is captured, so audit evidence for codes such as ISO 3834 and ASME IX is instant and undisputed.

Yes — effortlessly, from anywhere, even on mobile data, through the optional Remote Access Module.
