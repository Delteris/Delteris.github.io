What fabrication shops ask before moving weld traceability and welder qualification onto their own hardware.

What is welding management software?

Welding management software digitizes a fabrication shop's welding records — welder qualifications (WPS, PQR, WPQ), weld tracking and traceability, procedure control and inspection history — so every joint is traceable and audit-ready. Elenchon does this entirely on-premise, keeping the full weld record on hardware you own.

Elenchon is a 100% on-premise appliance installed in your facility — the sovereign alternative to cloud welding software (see the honest comparison). Your manufacturing data never touches a shared server, there are no forced updates, and an internet outage cannot stop production.

Does Elenchon manage welder qualification (WPS, PQR, WPQ)?

Yes. Elenchon runs the full WPS/PQR/WPQ qualification workflow with live WPQ capture, automatic 6-month welder continuity tracking and expiry alerts — so you never miss a requalification or weld outside an approved range.
