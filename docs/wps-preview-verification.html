The Feature That Makes a Parser Safe to Trust

WPS verification is checking each extracted field against the original document: Preview shows the parsed fields beside your genuine PDF or Excel form, so every read is confirmed against the page it came from — and any misread is corrected in the same view. Extraction saves the typing; verification is what lets you trust the result. Preview puts them together: the fields the app read on one side, the actual document your welding engineer signed on the other — the real PDF, page and all, or the Excel sheet as it is, not the app's re-interpretation. It's a workspace built to stay open while you work through a document, with a draggable divider to widen the source when a form is dense. And because compare and fix are the same screen, a correction — a value, a label, a flagged read marked verified, a computed heat input accepted — happens right where you spotted it, and updates everywhere it should.

The Genuine Document

The Real File, Not a Re-Render

You verify against the paperwork itself — the PDF or the Excel sheet, exactly as it is.

Checking a read against the app's own re-drawing of a form proves nothing. Preview shows you the actual source, so what you confirm against is the document of record.

  • The real PDF: A PDF WPS opens as the genuine PDF, page and all, with its own zoom and fit-to-width — the file your welding engineer signed, not an interpretation of it.
  • The real sheet: An Excel WPS renders as the actual sheet, drawn inline, so you're reading the workbook itself.
  • Field-to-cell confidence: With the parsed fields beside the source, your eye runs from a value to its place on the form and back — verification by direct comparison, not by faith.
Built to Work In

A Verification Workspace, Not a Glance

Preview stays open while you work a document — and gives you room when the form is dense.

Real verification means working through a whole document, not glimpsing it. Preview is built to live in for as long as the check takes.

  • Stays put: It won't vanish on a stray click outside it, so you can work down a document field by field without losing your place.
  • Room for a dense form: Widen the source pane with a draggable divider — up to half the screen — when a form is packed and you need to see it larger.
  • Made for the long look: The layout is designed for a sustained review of one document, not a quick peek that sends you back to a separate screen.
Compare and Fix, One View

Correct a Misread Without Leaving Preview

Edit the value, its label, or a flag right where you found the problem.

Spotting an error and fixing it shouldn't be two screens apart. In Preview, the correction happens in the same view as the comparison that revealed it.

  • Value or label, in place: Edit a misread value — or its label — right inside Preview and save, without bouncing to a separate edit screen.
  • Clear the flags: Tick a low-confidence field as verified, or accept a computed heat input, and the amber clears — the computed heat input's verify clears every pass at once.
  • Synced everywhere: A fix in Preview updates the library detail, the register column titles, and the review badge together — one correction, reflected wherever it belongs.

Questions & Answers

WPS Preview & Verification, Answered

What you check against, how it stays open, and how corrections propagate.

What does Preview show me?

A split screen: the fields the parser read on one side, your real document on the other — the genuine PDF rendered in place, or the Excel sheet drawn inline. You verify each read against the page it came from, in seconds.

Is it the real document or a re-render?

The real document. A PDF opens as the genuine PDF, page and all, with its own zoom and fit-to-width; an Excel WPS renders as the actual sheet. You're checking against the file your welding engineer signed, not the app's interpretation of it.

Can I fix a misread while I'm looking at it?

Yes. Edit the value or its label right inside Preview, tick a low-confidence field as verified, or accept a computed heat input — all without leaving the view. Compare and fix are the same screen.

Does a correction update the rest of the app?

Yes. A fix in Preview updates the library detail, the register column titles and the review badge together, so one correction is reflected everywhere it should be — no re-entering it elsewhere.

Will it stay open while I work through a form?

Yes. Preview is a verification workspace, not a modal that closes on a stray click. It stays put while you work a document field by field, and you can widen the source pane up to half the screen for a dense form.

How does it handle a heat input the app calculated?

A computed heat input is flagged for verification like any low-confidence read. Accept it in Preview and its verify clears the amber across every pass at once, so a single confirmation settles the whole calculation.
