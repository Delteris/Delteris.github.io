  2. PlannedQ1 2027 · Launch

    The Platform Goes Live on Your Floor

    Your team runs compliance tracking on real work, owned internally from day one, with offline-first capture included ahead of its original month-six plan. Full detail on the deployment page.

  3. PlannedMonths 1–5 · Refinement in real use

    The Product Bends to How You Actually Work

    When your feedback shapes Elenchon most: the floor app stays fast in real conditions, features adjust to how your team works, and new templates and export formats arrive as your paperwork needs them.

  4. PlannedMonth 6 · V2 release

    Two Outcomes, One Release

    The next major version delivers two things the floor has asked for. Standard-tier features arrive at no extra cost; Premium is an optional upgrade, priced openly at launch.

    Close the compliance loop downstream of the weld · Standard, included

    • Granular NDT trackingTest requests, results, and their link to the specific joint and repair cycle.
    • PWHT cycle trackingPost-weld heat treatment by total cycles and total cycle time, tied to the joints it covers.

    See and measure your production · Premium, optional

    • Productivity ManagerThroughput per worker, work type and project stage, with historical trends.
    • Visual Production TrackerA live map of the project: what’s moving now and where bottlenecks form, while there’s still time to act.

    Every plan, add-on, deployment and hardware price is on the pricing page, including Premium once its feature set is final.
