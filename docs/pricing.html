Common Questions

Pricing, Answered Before You Ask

The questions we get on every first call — answered here so you don’t have to make the call to get them.

When does billing start?

Not at deployment. Installation and training are the only charges up front; the monthly subscription begins after you're live. Your first monthly invoice lands on the 1st or the 15th — whichever falls furthest from your go-live date, so your first period is never short-changed. From there it's twelve invoices a year, one per calendar month. No 28-day cycles, no thirteenth invoice — the honest way to bill.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, and it costs nothing. Upgrades, downgrades and add-ons are applied remotely over the Phrakton fabric — give us 15 days’ notice. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.

Can one appliance run multiple factories?

A full factory, no — each one gets its own appliance. The app manages a single facility; it isn't built to run multiple shops from one box, so each site gets its own, with its records isolated and separately backed up. Two facilities, two appliances. The one exception: a genuinely small satellite operation may, at our discretion, run remote off an existing appliance — tell us before deployment and we'll scope it. Project, seasonal or field work isn't a satellite operation and is covered by the Remote Access Module.

How large a floor does Lite cover?

Lite includes 20 workers and takes up to two expansion blocks of ten — a 40-worker ceiling. Past that you move to Standard, which includes 50 and expands without limit. Moving up is a remote plan change, not a re-installation.

What comes with a worker block?

€90 per month adds ten workers, and every block carries 20 GB of recurring remote bandwidth at no extra charge. If you need more than that, bandwidth is €25 per 100 GB.

Will we need to buy bandwidth?

Almost certainly not. The bandwidth included with the Remote Access Module and each worker block is sized for normal remote use — a floor working over the fabric day to day stays comfortably inside it. Extra bandwidth exists for genuine outliers, not the everyday case; unless you're moving remote data far beyond ordinary work, the included allowance is enough and you never touch a pack.

What does installation cost?

Standard is €1,700 all in — €850 deployment and €850 floor training, quoted as one line. Lite is a single €950 package covering both, lower for its one on-site trip versus two. Extra training sessions are €500. Hardware is passed through at cost — roughly €300–€400 — with zero markup.

What if the fabric goes down?

The fabric is the access path to your appliance, so we treat its uptime as our responsibility and back it in writing. We commit to 99.9% monthly and engineer for as near 100% as possible; on any shortfall the credit is automatic — 10% below 99.9% uptime, 25% below 99%, 50% below 97%, and the full month free below 89.9%.

There is no lock-in and no exit fee. The appliance is yours and the data never left it, so you keep view-only access to your historical records for audits.

How We Think About Pricing

Priced Fairly, Not Maximally

This industry has room to overcharge, and plenty of software takes it — pricing to the maximum a buyer will bear, then hiding the number behind a sales call. We don't think a healthy margin entitles us to that. We set our price at what we believe is fair for a complete platform, not the most we could squeeze out, and we publish it in full so you can hold us to it.
